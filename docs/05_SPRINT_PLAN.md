## Ordering principles

1. **Cross-cutting first.** Tenant resolution, audit log, i18n/RTL and design tokens exist before the first screen (D-06, D-14).
2. **Onboarding before company features.** Self-registration with a subdomain, payment proof and System Owner verification replaces a plain "Create Company" (D-06, D-07, D-19).
3. **Engines before their consumers.** Platform services → Action engine → Inspection; Risk Assessment → Permit activation (review §4).
4. **Offline groundwork before offline features.** The sync layer is built before Inspection and Safety Event capture (D-11, D-20).

## Notes

- **Approval steps vs short status lists (D-12).** The Master Rules statuses omit "Under Review" / "Verification" / "Suspended". Required approvals are modelled as approval records on the workflow, not as statuses. To confirm when Sprints 8, 10 and 13 are specified.
- **Phone verification (Sprint 2)** needs an SMS provider, which the Product Book lists as out of scope (F-07). If none is chosen, Sprint 2 ships with email verification only.
- **Story numbering:** US-064–068 is used both for Dashboard & Reports (Doc 14) and for Toolbox Talk (Doc 24). This plan uses Doc 14's numbering until Q-28 is answered.
