**Status: DONE — accepted by the Product Owner on 2026-11-28 (D-64).** Approved by the Product Owner on 2026-11-27 (D-62), including technical decisions T-84 to T-93 and the proposed answers to open points O-156 to O-182 (D-63). Safety events are the **second consumer** of the Sprint 9 offline layer (D-11: hazard reporting) and the second source module of the Sprint 8 action engine.

## Goal

"Every safety-related occurrence is treated as a **Safety Event**. The event type determines the workflow, not the database structure" (Doc 11 §2). This sprint builds **one** engine for hazards, unsafe acts and conditions, near misses and incidents (Doc 11 §19: "Never create separate modules for hazards and incidents"):

- **reporting** that is fast on a phone, **with or without a connection, including photos** (D-11), by anyone who works on the project, workers included;
- **event types and categories as company data**, each type carrying its rules (form sections, mandatory investigation, minimum severity), never code (Doc 11 §3–4);
- a **review** step where the safety team confirms type, severity and the **risk rating** from the company's **risk matrix** (Doc 11 §12, Q-22), then decides: investigate, act, or close;
- **investigations** separate from the report (Doc 11 §19): an investigator and team, immediate causes, root causes, contributing factors, recommendations, lessons learned (Doc 11 §9), and **witnesses** with statements (§10);
- **corrective actions** through `ActionEngine`, linked to the event for ever (Doc 11 §11, §17);
- **closure** after the actions are done and verified (Doc 20 "Event Closure"), after which the event is read-only.

## Definition of Done

- Every acceptance criterion in the nine specs passes, including the six **Doc 11 §17** and six **Doc 20** business rules as integration tests (one company, one project, one type per event; investigation required for high severity; investigation only after the event is reported; actions linked to the originating event; closed events read-only; no hard delete — voiding only).
- **Workflow tests**: every allowed and refused transition of `SAFETY_EVENT_WORKFLOW` with the right permission; required investigation can't be skipped (type rule and severity rule); self-closure refused; two reviewers racing → one wins, the other gets `SAFETY_EVENT_TRANSITION_NOT_ALLOWED`.
- **Offline tests** (API integration, Sprint 9 helpers): report + 3 photos sent twice → one event, one number, no duplicate files; reporter edit offline after a reviewer opened it → refused into Needs attention (`SAFETY_EVENT_LOCKED`); reporter edit offline and a reviewer's classification online → `SYNC_CONFLICT` only for the same field; a report on an inactive type within the 7-day window accepted.
- **Risk matrix tests**: `rateRisk` for 5×5 and 4×4 (web unit); invalid bands refused (gaps, overlaps); a matrix edit leaves existing ratings unchanged (snapshot).
- **Sensitive data tests**: a viewer without `sensitive.view` gets the event without people, medical files or their history entries; the lead investigator gets them; removed from the team → no longer.
- **Actions**: actions raised at review and from recommendations in one batch get consecutive numbers; closing the last one moves the event to Verification; voiding one re-opens its recommendation and the event goes back to Corrective Action.
- Matrix test extended with the eight permissions; read-only route review (PLT-11) lists every new write.
- Browser check in **Arabic and English at 375 px**, demo company, on the **production build** for the offline part: Company Administrator reviews the default risk matrix → a **Worker, offline**, reports a hazard with two photos and their location → back online it syncs and gets its number; the Safety Officer reviews it, rates it High on the matrix, which makes the investigation required, and assigns a Site Engineer → the Site Engineer records two witnesses, causes with a 5 Whys chain and two recommendations, raising one action, and completes the investigation → the action owner closes the action (verified) → the event shows **Verification** → the HSE Manager closes it with a summary → the Worker sees **Closed** and the summary in My reports. Plus an incident with an injured person viewed by a Client (no injury details shown).
- `npm run check` and `npm run build` pass; migrations apply to a fresh local D1 and on top of the Sprint 10 data.
- `03_DATABASE.md`, `04_API.md`, the module READMEs (`modules/safety-event/README.md`, `modules/risk/README.md`) and `modules/offline/README.md` (second consumer) are updated.
