# Sprint 7 — Workforce

**Status: DONE — accepted by the Product Owner on 2026-11-14 (D-51).** Approved by the Product Owner on 2026-11-14 (D-49), including technical decisions T-47 to T-56 and the proposed answers to open points O-71 to O-95 (D-50). O-71 answers **Q-31** for workforce files.

## Goal

Every person who works on the company's sites gets **one workforce profile** (Doc 09 §3, §19): employees, contractors, subcontractors, temporary workers, visitors, consultants and client representatives, with or without a login (D-09). Safety teams assign people to projects, keep their training, certificates, medical fitness, emergency contacts and personal documents, and are told **before anything expires**. From Sprint 8 onwards, inspections, permits, toolbox talks and risk assessments can name the people involved and later check that they are fit and qualified (Doc 18 "Required by").

## Technical decisions (proposed)

| ID   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T-47 | A new **`workforce` module** with **one `workforce_member` table** for every category (a `category` column, Doc 09 §19). The profile holds no project data; assignments, training, certificates, medical, contacts and documents are child tables. Later modules use only the module's contract: `WorkforceDirectory.resolve(memberIds)`, `search(…)`, `availability(memberId, projectId, onDate)` → status, assignment, compliance summary. | Doc 09 §19: unified model, profile independent of assignments, modular services. Later modules never read workforce tables directly (T-35).                                         |
| T-48 | Training, certificate, medical and document records are **never deleted**. A wrong entry is **edited** (audited, old and new values) or **voided** with a reason (hidden by default, shown under "Show voided"). A renewal is a **new record** with `replaces_id` pointing to the old one, which then shows as "Replaced". Only emergency contacts are deleted (they are contact data, not history).                                         | Doc 09 §8 "historical training records should never be deleted", §17 "history must remain available for audits"; renewals keep the earlier certificate visible to auditors.         |
| T-49 | Three new **file categories**: `workforce_photo` (images), `workforce_attachment` (training and certificate copies), `workforce_personal` (documents and medical attachments). They count against the plan's storage (D-28). Workforce files are served **only through workforce routes** that check the record's permission; the generic `GET /files/{id}` returns `404` for these categories.                                              | The generic file route only checks `company.view`; a passport copy must need `workforce.personal.view` (O-75). One upload service, as Master Rules §5 requires.                     |
| T-50 | **Expiry state is computed, not stored**: one shared function `expiryState(expiresOn, today)` → `none`, `valid`, `expiring` (within 30 days, O-83), `expired`, where `today` is the calendar date in the company's time zone. Records store `expires_on` as a date (indexed); lists filter by date ranges. The profile's **compliance summary** (PLT-20) is computed from current records on each request.                                   | A stored status would go stale every midnight and need a job to repair it; the same function will serve equipment, permits and documents later.                                     |
| T-51 | A new daily job **`workforce.expiry`** finds records reaching 30 days, 7 days and the expiry day, and assignments ending in 7 days. It sends **one digest notification per recipient per day** ("6 workforce items need attention") linking to the filtered directory, plus a personal notification to the **linked user** about their own records. Each record and threshold is sent once (`ReminderService` key).                          | A company with 500 workers would otherwise get dozens of notifications a day; one digest keeps the bell useful. Reminder keys make a missed or repeated run safe.                   |
| T-52 | Workforce search uses a stored **`search_key`**: both names, the employee ID and the employer, lower-cased, with Arabic letters normalised (alef and hamza forms → ا, ى → ي, ة → ه, diacritics and tatweel removed). Search is a `LIKE` on this column inside the company, combined with indexed filters (status, category, job position, nationality, project), 25 results per page.                                                        | Fast enough for tens of thousands of profiles per company in D1 without a search service; Arabic users find "احمد" when the name was typed "أحمد". Answers Q-35 for workforce only. |
| T-53 | The **user link** (D-09) uses the existing `user.workforce_member_id`: a unique index (one profile per user, one user per profile), checked by the service, **no database foreign key** (as for `company.logo_file_id`, D-39, and `user_role.project_id`, D-45). Archiving a profile removes the link.                                                                                                                                       | SQLite can't add a foreign key to an existing column without rebuilding the `user` table; the service already owns the rule.                                                        |
| T-54 | **Job positions and competencies** live in one company-level table `workforce_list_item` with a `list` column. Every company gets the Doc 09 §6 and §7 examples as **seeded items** that keep a translation key until someone renames them, so they show in each reader's language. New companies are seeded at creation; existing companies by migration.                                                                                   | One table, one service and one settings screen for both lists; seeded items stay bilingual without asking the company to translate them.                                            |
| T-55 | **Assignments** (`workforce_assignment`) are never deleted. Their state is derived from dates: **Upcoming**, **Current**, **Ended**, or **Cancelled** (an upcoming assignment withdrawn by mistake). One person can't have two overlapping assignments on the **same** project; assignments on different projects may overlap.                                                                                                               | Doc 09 §5 "assignments should remain historically traceable", §17 "historical assignments must remain available for audits".                                                        |
| T-56 | Changes to a profile and its records are written to the **activity timeline** with `project_id` empty and `entity_type = workforce_member` (shown as the profile's **History** tab); assignment changes are also written to the project's timeline. Entries about documents and medical records are shown only to readers with `workforce.personal.view`.                                                                                    | Reuses the Sprint 6 timeline (T-43) instead of a second history mechanism; sensitive changes don't leak through history.                                                            |

## Definition of Done

- Every acceptance criterion in the nine specs passes.
- **Matrix test** extended with the seven new permissions (O-73–O-77) at company and project scope, including company adjustments.
- **Sensitive data tests:** without `workforce.personal.view` no API response contains document numbers, medical notes or personal file links, and the file routes return `404`; without `workforce.contacts.view` no contact data; the generic `/files/{id}` returns `404` for every workforce category; every personal document opening writes an audit entry.
- **Expiry tests:** `expiryState` table-driven around the 30-day and expiry-day boundaries in two time zones; the daily job sends each threshold once, one digest per recipient, the right recipients only (O-84), and nothing for voided or replaced records.
